### 👋 About

I'm a **Computer Science student at Pamantasan ng Cabuyao (Class of 2027)** with a deep passion for software engineering, community building, and getting lost in a good book. I believe the best systems aren't just built with clean code—they're built by collaborative, inclusive communities.

Whether I'm architecting a full-stack project or helping cultivate a space for developers to connect and grow, my goal is always to build things that last. 

- 🤝 **Community Builder:** Driven to foster collaboration, share knowledge, and create environments where tech enthusiasts can thrive together.
- 🧱 **System Architecture:** I care deeply about **clean code**, **maintainability**, and technical decisions you can defend.
- 📚 **Avid Reader:** Always analyzing tightly-engineered narratives, whether it's a gripping crime thriller or brilliant technical documentation.
- 📝 **Documentation Advocate:** A strong believer that **good specs** prevent most bad sprints.
